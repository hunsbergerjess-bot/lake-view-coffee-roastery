Our Story

More Than Just Coffee

Lake View Coffee Roastery was built around a simple idea: exceptional coffee should be produced with care for the people and communities behind it.

We prioritize ethically sourced beans and locally roasted coffee. Our goal is to create a thoughtful coffee experience that brings together quality, craftsmanship, and community.

"Great coffee begins with great care."

Our Approach

We believe that the choices made throughout the coffee supply chain matter.
From sourcing to roasting, each step contributes to the final experience.

Learn more about responsible coffee sourcing through the Specialty Coffee Association.
